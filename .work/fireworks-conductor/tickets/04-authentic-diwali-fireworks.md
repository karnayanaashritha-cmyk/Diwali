# Ticket 04: Authentic Diwali Fireworks Systems

## Intent
Implement the 7 distinct pyrotechnic firework behaviors mapped to user hand gestures with authentic Indian Diwali firework characteristics.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] **1. Rocket (☝️ Index Up):** Launches upward streak from fingertip with sparks, peaks at screen top, and explodes into an aerial peony or golden willow burst.
- [ ] **2. Anar / Fountain (✊ Fist):** Sustained vertical cone fountain spraying glittering golden-white sparks upward from top of fist with gravity fallout and crackle sparks.
- [ ] **3. Starburst Aerial Shell (✋ Open Palm):** Explosive spherical burst radiating outward from palm center with multi-colored sparks and gentle downward curvature.
- [ ] **4. Roman Candle (✌️ Peace Sign):** Periodic sequential colored fireballs shooting upward from fingers (Ruby, Emerald, Gold, Sapphire) with trail sparks.
- [ ] **5. Phooljhadi / Sparkler (👌 Pinch):** Continuous crackling sparkler following pinch coordinates, emitting branching jitter sparks for drawing in the air.
- [ ] **6. Chakri (🔄 Circle Trace):** High-speed rotating ring of sparks spinning at circle origin, throwing spiral tangential embers.
- [ ] **7. Grand Finale (🙌 Both Palms Open):** Staggered multi-shell bombardment across the entire screen filling the night sky with golden brocade willows.
